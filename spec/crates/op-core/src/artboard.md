# artboard.rs

Artboards are top-level group containers with an exclusive document-space rectangle and white, black, or transparent background. `Artboard::valid` rejects empty or excessive sizes and coordinates. `create` allocates an Artboard N group, optionally nests intersecting loose raster layers by content center unless nesting/all-locked, converts nested backgrounds to regular layers, and expands the canvas. `at` hits visible board rectangles topmost first. `set` changes the clipping rectangle; moving translates descendants and masks, resizing retains all child pixels. Position locks block changes. Canvas expansion preserves image coordinates by shifting all layers and board rectangles together. History belongs to callers; snapshots include board metadata. Tests cover clipping, hit testing, retained pixels on resizing, child movement, expansion, snapshot restoration, locks and invalid geometry. Adobe reference parity for nesting rules and presets remains unmeasured.

Creating an artboard converts Background layers to regular layers so canvas expansion remains transparent. `auto_nest` reparents selected root blocks into/out of boards according to content center, preserving contiguous group blocks, selected IDs and nesting/all locks. Ordinary group members remain in their group. Negative expansion, blank-board background/Move and auto-nesting have regression tests.
