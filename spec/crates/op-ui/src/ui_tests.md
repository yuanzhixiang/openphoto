# ui_tests.rs: windowless UI tests

## Responsibilities

Uses egui_kittest to run the whole application (including the wgpu canvas) without a window, to simulate input, inspect state, and render the screen to PNG for pixel-by-pixel comparison with Photoshop screenshots. Compiled only in tests.

## Conventions

- `harness(files)`: starts the application at Photoshop's default window size (1350×800 pt) without installing the native macOS menu (`OpenPhotoApp::new_headless`). Because there is no native menu, ⌘ shortcuts are handled by egui (see `commands.md`). Each step advances 1/60 second (kittest's default is 1/4 second), matching the real frame interval, so that a double-click falls within egui's 0.3 second double-click window.
- The pixel density is set to 2 physical pixels per pt, matching the Retina screen the Photoshop reference screenshots were taken on, so the canvas size at 100% zoom also matches.
- `shot(harness, name)`: writes the current screen to `target/ui-shots/{name}.png` (2700×1600), and also writes a copy scaled down to 1 pixel per pt, `{name}_1x.png` (1350×800), whose coordinates correspond exactly to a 1:1 screenshot of the Photoshop window, so it can be cropped and compared directly.
- `reference_document(harness)`: closes all documents and opens a document with the same size and file name as the one in the Photoshop reference screenshots (734×811, no embedded profile), used for screenshot comparison.
- `click(harness, pos)`: simulates one left click at a position. `at_pt(x, y)` converts Photoshop points to egui coordinates.

## Tests

- Regular tests (run by `cargo test`): verify interaction and state:
  - Clicking the foreground color swatch in the toolbar opens the Color Picker; Esc cancels without changing the color.
  - Dragging with the Rectangular Marquee produces a selection of the matching extent and records history; Shift-drag adds to the selection; a single click deselects; ⇧⌘D reselects, ⌘A selects all, ⌘D deselects.
  - ⇧M switches to the Elliptical Marquee; pressing M afterward still selects the Elliptical Marquee (the slot remembers it).
  - B selects the Brush; `]` increases 30 px to 35 px; number keys change the opacity; after dragging one stroke, the pixels it passed over become the foreground color while the others do not change, and "Brush Tool" is recorded.
  - Clicking with the Brush while the layer is hidden shows Photoshop's alert.
  - ⌥⌫ and ⌘⌫ fill the selection with the foreground and background colors; on a regular layer ⌫ clears the selection; with no selection ⌫ deletes the layer.
  - ⇧F5 opens the Fill dialog; Enter fills with the foreground color.
  - A single click with the Paint Bucket fills the whole contiguous area and records "Paint Bucket".
  - The Move tool drags a colored block on a layer; arrow keys and Shift+arrow keys nudge it, recording "Move" and "Nudge" respectively; dragging the background layer with no selection shows the lock alert.
  - After clicking the Auto-Select checkbox in the options bar, with an empty upper layer as the current layer, dragging a block on a lower layer automatically selects and moves the lower layer; after turning on Show Transform Controls, dragging a corner of the box enters Free Transform.
  - After filling the selection with red, ⌘C and paste (⌘V is simulated with the `Event::Paste` that egui-winit actually sends): produces "Layer 1", stacked at the original position, the selection is deselected, and "Paste" is recorded; ⌘X on the new layer cuts away half and records "Cut"; copying an area containing only transparent pixels shows the "selected area is empty" alert.
  - ⌘J on the background layer produces "Layer 1" and on a regular layer produces "Layer 1 copy"; ⌘[ moves it back one level and records "Layer Order"; ⌘E merges down; ⇧⌘E merges all visible layers.
  - In the Layers panel, dragging the top row to the middle changes the order and records "Layer Order"; dragging below the background layer has no effect; double-clicking the name, typing a new name, and pressing Enter records "Rename Layer"; clicking the background layer's lock icon turns the background into "Layer 0".
  - After rotating 90° clockwise, width and height swap and "Rotate Canvas" is recorded; with no selection Crop is unavailable, with a selection it crops to the selection; after painting a red block on a white background, Trim (by default based on the top-left color) crops to the red block.
  - ⌘I inverts (#141414 → #ebebeb) and records "Invert"; opening Threshold immediately previews the document as white, Esc cancels and restores it, and history is unchanged; Posterize applies with the default 4 levels on Enter and records "Posterize".
  - ⌘L opens Levels; typing 20 into the already focused input black point previews the document as black; Enter records "Levels".
  - Gaussian Blur applied to a white square (type 4 into the focused radius field, Enter) turns the edges into intermediate values; Last Filter is unavailable before, and afterwards ⌃⌘F applies it again and adds another "Gaussian Blur" history entry.
  - After a modification, the tab title ends with " *"; after `save_to` saves as PSD, the title becomes the new file name and the "*" disappears; undoing to before the save counts as modified, redoing back does not; after a modification, F12 reverts to the file contents and records "Revert"; closing a modified document asks for confirmation, after Esc cancels the document is still there, and after ⌘D (Don't Save) the document closes.
  - A single click with the Eyedropper picks red as the foreground color, ⌥-click picks the background color, and a 5×5 average straddling a block corner yields an intermediate color; a single click with the Magic Wand on the block gives a selection of the block (the outer ring is half-selected due to anti-aliasing); clicking three corners with the Polygonal Lasso and pressing Enter creates a triangular selection; dragging a triangular path with the Lasso creates a selection.
  - G selects the Gradient tool; dragging black to white from x=100 to x=600 makes the left end black, the right end white, and the middle about 128, and records "Gradient" (also produces the `gradient` screenshot).
  - On the background layer with no selection, ⌘T shows the lock alert; on a new layer, after ⌘T, dragging inside the box shows the preview immediately and Enter records "Free Transform"; ⌘T again, dragging the bottom-right handle to scale up about 2×, then Esc leaves the layer unchanged; ⇧⌘T translates once more and records "Transform Again" (also produces the `free_transform` screenshot).
  - C selects the Crop tool and the crop box covers the whole canvas; after dragging the bottom-right handle to (500, 500) and pressing Enter, the canvas is about 500×500, "Crop" is recorded, and the box again covers the new canvas; after switching to M the crop box disappears (also produces the `crop` screenshot).
  - ⌥⌘I opens Image Size; typing 367 into the already focused width makes the height proportionally 406; Enter makes the document 367×406 and records "Image Size" (also produces the `image_size` screenshot).
  - ⌘R shows rulers; dragging from the top ruler into the canvas creates a horizontal guide and records "New Guide"; ⌘' shows the grid (produces the `rulers` screenshot); the Move tool drags the guide down and records "Move Guide"; dragging it back onto the ruler deletes it and records "Delete Guide", and ⌘Z restores it; ⌥⌘; locks guides.
  - With no selection Expand is unavailable; a 100..140 selection expanded by 5 becomes 95..145 and records "Expand" (produces the `expand_selection` screenshot); ⇧F6 opens Feather; on a solid-color document Similar selects the whole image.
  - O Dodge lightens the dark gray background and records "Dodge Tool"; S Clone Stamp shows an alert when no sample point is set, and after ⌥-clicking the red block, clicking elsewhere paints red; Y History Brush restores that spot to the color it had when opened.
  - On a red layer, Hide Selection creates a mask, the selected area reveals the background, and "Add Layer Mask" is recorded (produces the `layer_mask` screenshot); filling black on the mask hides more while the layer pixels are still red; disabling the mask shows everything; after Apply the mask disappears and the corresponding pixels become transparent.
  - With a selection, pressing Q enters Quick Mask, records "Quick Mask", the title contains "(Quick Mask/8", and the area outside the selection is shown in red; after painting black inside the selection with the Brush and pressing Q again, the result is a selection that excludes that spot (produces the `quick_mask` screenshot).
  - U selects the Rectangle tool; dragging creates "Rectangle 1" and records "Rectangle Tool"; ⇧U switches to Ellipse, and dragging while holding ⌥ creates "Ellipse 1" centered on the press point (produces the `shapes` screenshot).
  - T selects the Type tool; after a single click, typing "Hello", Enter, "World" — single-key shortcuts do not take effect meanwhile, and the layer shows "Hello" live (produces the `type_tool` screenshot); after ⌘Enter "Type Tool" is recorded and there are white pixels above the baseline; starting another paragraph and pressing Esc after typing discards the text.
  - ⌘N opens the New dialog (produces the `new_document` screenshot); Enter creates a 1920×1080 "Untitled-2"; Transparent background contents creates a regular "Layer 1" layer, with the resolution as set.
  - On a red background, ⌥⇧⌘B opens Black & White and applying the default preset gives 102 gray; Gradient Map uses black to white; opens Photo Filter (produces the `photo_filter` screenshot).
  - ⌘M opens Curves; clicking at (128, 192) on the curve graph adds a point (produces the `curves` screenshot); after Enter, 128 gray becomes about 192 and "Curves" is recorded.
  - F8 opens Info, then Navigator and Histogram are opened, and the three panels do not overlap (produces the `floating_panels` screenshot); hovering records the pointer's document coordinates; `center_on` changes the view; pressing F8 again closes Info.
