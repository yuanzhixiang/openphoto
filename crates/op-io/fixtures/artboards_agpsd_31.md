# Independent artboard PSD fixture

`artboards_agpsd_31.psd` was generated with ag-psd 31.0.3, using its `writePsdBuffer` API, without Photoshop, desktop input or capture. It is 40×20 with four empty, expanded group containers. Each board is 8×8 at y=2 and x=0, 10, 20, 30; their backgrounds are White (type 1), Black (2), Transparent (3), and custom RGB(20,30,40) (4). The document artboard descriptor reports four boards and a white default background.

This fixture validates the standard descriptor reader against an independent writer. It is not a file saved by Photoshop and does not establish live Photoshop parity. Descriptor source: https://github.com/Agamnentzar/ag-psd/blob/master/src/additionalInfo.ts
